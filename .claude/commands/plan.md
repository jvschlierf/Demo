Task: $ARGUMENTS

Before writing any code:
1. Restate the requirement in your own words.
2. List assumptions and open questions (ask me the important ones).
3. List edge cases and how they should be handled.
4. Propose a minimal design: modules, functions with signatures, data structures.
5. Propose the test cases you'll write.

Then stop and wait for my OK.
